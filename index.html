FAQ

Is New Method Driving School approved by the MTO?
Yes. New Method Driving School is fully MTO-approved and licensed. This means our Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course meets MTO standards and allows students to qualify for insurance discounts and earlier G2 road test eligibility.
What is the BDE course?
BDE stands for Beginner Driver Education. It is a comprehensive course that includes 10 hours of online learning and 20 hours of in-car training.
How long do I need to wait for my G2 test?
With a valid BDE certificate, you can take your G2 test after 8 months instead of 12 months.
Where do I get my driver education certificate from?
We provide the certificate upon successful completion of the course.
How do I book my road test?
You can book your road test online through the DriveTest website, or our instructors can guide you through the process.
Is the BDE course self-paced?
Yes, the online portion of the BDE course is self-paced, allowing you to complete it on your own schedule.
How are in-car lessons scheduled?
In-car lessons are scheduled directly with your instructor based on mutual availability.
How many driving lessons are completed per week?
This varies based on the student's availability and package, but typically students complete 1-2 lessons per week.
Can I choose my preferred days for lessons?
Absolutely. We strive to accommodate your schedule and will match you with an instructor who is available on your preferred days.
Is the pricing on your website up to date?
Yes, the pricing listed on our website is our current standard pricing.
