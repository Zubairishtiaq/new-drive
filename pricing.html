Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers about BDE courses, driving lessons, road tests, certificates and payments.

New Method Driving School offers an MTO-approved Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course. Contact the school if you need help confirming course details or eligibility before registering.
BDE stands for Beginner Driver Education. It combines driver education with practical training to help new drivers build knowledge, safe habits and confidence behind the wheel.
The Silver package is listed at $565 plus HST and includes 30 hours of online instruction, 10 hours of in-car lessons, free pick-up and drop-off, and the listed BDE-related benefits.
Silver is the essential package. Gold adds use of the instructor's vehicle for the road test. Platinum includes 12 hours of in-car lessons and the instructor's vehicle for the road test. Review each package above for the full list of inclusions.
G2 lessons start at $45 plus HST for one hour, with 5-hour and 10-hour options also available. G lessons start at $50 plus HST for one hour. The instructor's vehicle for a G2/G test is listed at $125 plus HST.
You can discuss your preferred days and times when booking. Lesson times depend on instructor availability, so booking ahead is recommended.
Free pick-up and drop-off are listed as inclusions in the Silver, Gold and Platinum BDE packages. Confirm your address and availability with the school before booking.
Yes, use of the instructor's vehicle for the road test is included in the Gold and Platinum packages. It is also available as a separately listed service for $125 plus HST, subject to confirmation.
An eligible BDE course can allow a G1 driver to take the G2 road test earlier than the standard waiting period. Confirm your eligibility and current requirements with DriveTest or the relevant Ontario authority.
Some insurers may offer discounts to eligible drivers who complete an approved driver education course. Discounts vary by insurer and policy, so check directly with your insurance provider.
The online portion is designed to be completed online. Ask the school about access, course scheduling and any completion deadlines before you start.
Course completion documentation is provided after you successfully complete the required course components and the school has processed your completion. Ask the school if you need a specific timeline.
Road tests can be booked through Ontario's official DriveTest service. Your instructor may also be able to guide you through the process.
Call New Method Driving School at 905-922-2544 to ask about package availability, lesson scheduling, pick-up locations and payment details.
Prices shown on this page are listed before HST. Please confirm the final total and accepted payment methods with the school when booking.
